The house

A working inn, not a stage set.

Hearth & Harbor has taken in weather-blown travelers since 1912. Fires are laid at four. Breakfast is served until the last guest comes down. Both the public rooms and the innkeeper’s ledger live in this house — book a chamber, then find it again by name.
